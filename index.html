Enrolment

Course registration

Fields marked * are required
1Create your free Skills account
  1. Go to skills.google and click Join.
  2. Sign up with the same email ID you enter in this form.
  3. Open the verification email and confirm your account.
2Get your public profile link
  1. Log in and open skills.google/profile.
  2. Click Make profile public (confirm if asked).
  3. Copy the link from the address bar. It looks like skills.google/public_profiles/…

Watch the 1-minute video guide ↗

3Which screenshots to upload
  1. Email ID screenshot — open skills.google/my_account/profile and capture the Email field. It must be the same email ID you type in this form.
  2. Profile link screenshot — your public profile page with the link visible in the address bar (…/public_profiles/…), or the green box “Your profile is public and accessible at …” on the same settings page.
Your email ID, profile link and screenshot are verified automatically. Only verified registrations are accepted.
